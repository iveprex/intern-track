## InternTrack — CareerTech Application Workspace

A self-initiated CareerTech product concept designed to help students organize internship opportunities and manage applications from discovery through outcome.

**Highlights**
- Opportunity search and filtering
- Application pipeline
- Deadline tracking
- Status management
- Career analytics
- Skill and work-mode insights

**Role:** Product concept · Developer  
**Stack:** Python, Streamlit, Pandas  
**Data:** Fictional demonstration dataset
